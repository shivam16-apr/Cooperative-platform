# Project Demo

## Cooperative Gig Services Platform

The working prototype demonstration of the Cooperative Gig Services Platform is available at the link below:

[Watch Project Demo](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1RlDsyp20DXwv0BYLjDpWq0bu4_IQCVTj/view?usp=drivesdk)

### Demo Highlights

The demonstration showcases the major workflows of the platform:

- Customer registration and login
- Service selection and booking
- Worker selection and booking confirmation
- Location sharing and service tracking
- Worker dashboard and job management
- Admin dashboard and platform management
- Demand forecasting and workforce allocation
- Worker welfare and insurance management
